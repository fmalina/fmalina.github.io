Welcome. I am František. Fixer, software engineer, creator of Unilexicon taxonomy editor, sometimes I write software and upgrade ageing systems into snappy working products. Between 2009–2016 I run Flatmate Rooms, nationwide room rental site, later Be Local web.

I specialise in taking neglected, slow or overly complex web systems and making them simpler, faster and easier to maintain.

You'll find me at home sleeves up doing some woodwork, carpentry, refurbishing remote cabins and mountain bothies, writing websites and software. I run a small bed and breakfast. Come, visit.

Originally from Slovakia, I worked for 15 years in England and went back home. My CV.
