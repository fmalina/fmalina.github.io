BRYNDZOVE TYČINKY

A Christmas staple, we bake these every year! They are super easy to make and are an absolute treat. We used the recipe from @kauflandslovensko and it never fails to disappoint. They are the perfect snack and I love them. I urge you to try them! Recipe (credits to @kauflandslovensko ) is as follows:

- 250g flour + extra for dusting
- 250g bryndza (Slovak sheep’s cheese)
- 250g butter
- 1 egg + 1 egg for egg wash
- Caraway seeds

Method :
1. Mix the flour, butter, bryndza and 1 egg in a bowl. Mix until it forms a dough.
2. Cut into 4.
3. Roll out to about 5mm thick.
4. Cut into strips or rounds and brush with egg wash and sprinkle with caraway seeds. (Repeat with rest of the dough)
5. Bake at 180C for 10-15 mins or until golden brown.
6. Allow to cool and enjoy!
