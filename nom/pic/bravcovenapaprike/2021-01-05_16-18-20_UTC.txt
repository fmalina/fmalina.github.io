Slovenský raj / Slovak Paradise national park, Jan. 3–5, 2004. Incredible 17 years ago. On that occasion I seriously started hiking.
(1) Zajfy, a crossroads between Stratená and Dedinky villages. My father, Róbert, demonstrates that when hunger is so overwhelming, even canned mash of ground meat and pepper ("Bravčové na paprike") is a delicious lunch course.
(2) At one of the several ladder sections in the Zejmarská gorge near Biele Vody, Mlynky; together with Rudolf, my brother, who would be my companion on many such hikes over the subsequent decade.
