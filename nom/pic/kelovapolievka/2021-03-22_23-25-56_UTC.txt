One of my childhood favourite soups 😋 Savoy cabbage soup with carrots, potato and chorizo crumb! 🥕🥔
This big plate of soup is only 140 calories!!! (Obviously we wanted to add the fresh sourdough bread 🍞🥰).
I normally make this soup with frankfurters, but found chorizo crumb at @waitrose and a tiny bit goes a long way - gives it very full/spicy flavour. Loved it!
