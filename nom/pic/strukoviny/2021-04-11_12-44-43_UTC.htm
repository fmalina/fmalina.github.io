😋 Lentils with indian spices blend #chanadalmasala 😋Sosovica s indickym korenim
📝
Recipe /Recept:
- 2 onions (2cibule)
- 1 package, about 300 g of cherry tomatos (1 balenie cherry paradajok)
- 500 g yellow lentils (500 g zltej sosovice)
- 10-15 g spices Chana dal Madala (10-15g korenia Chana dal Masala)
- olive oil (olivovy olej)
***
Cook lentils. Fry chopped onion in oil, add chopped tomatoes and spice Chana dal Masala. Stir for 5-10 minutes, till it becomes paste. Add one part of boiled lentils, mix well and cook another 5 minutes. Transfer it to the remaining lentils.
(Uvar sosovicu. Opraz nakrajanu cibulu v oleji, pridaj nakrajane paradajky a korenie Chana dal Masala. Miesaj 5-10 min., kym z toho nebude kasa. Pridaj cast uvarenej sosovice, premiesaj a var dalsich 5 min. Presun to a vmiesaj do zvysku sosovice.)
